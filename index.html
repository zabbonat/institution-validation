Instructions and category definitions

For each institution choose one type and one ownership, using the links to check what the organisation is. Classify the organisation as it is described today. If the available information is not sufficient, choose Cannot tell. Progress is saved in this browser, so the page can be closed and reopened. When finished, or at any time, download the results file and send it back by email.

Keyboard: 1 to 7 for the type, Q W E R for the ownership, Enter for the next institution, the left arrow for the previous one.

University
Higher education institution that grants degrees, including its faculties and schools.
Research institute
Organisation whose main activity is research and that does not grant degrees: public laboratories, non-profit institutes, corporate research laboratories registered as separate bodies.
Hospital
Hospital, clinic, medical centre or health system whose main activity is patient care, including university hospitals.
Company
For-profit firm.
Government agency
Ministry, department, regulator or other body of the public administration whose main activity is not research.
Other
Any other organisation: museums, libraries, archives, foundations and funders, associations, NGOs, schools.
Public
Owned, controlled or mainly funded by the state or by another public body.
Private
Owned or controlled by private parties, for-profit or non-profit.
Hybrid
Mixed public and private ownership or control, for example public-private partnerships and joint ventures.
